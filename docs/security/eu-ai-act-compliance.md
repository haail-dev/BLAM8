# EU AI Act — BLAM8 gap analysis

*Prepared 3 July 2026. Legal position verified against the Commission's draft Article 50
guidelines (8 May 2026) and current commentary. Not legal advice — a working comparison
for prioritising compliance work. Re-check when the final Article 50 guidelines and the
Code of Practice on AI-generated content land (expected before 2 Aug 2026).*

---

## 1. Does the Act apply to BLAM8 at all?

**Yes, whenever BLAM8 serves the EU.** BLAM8 (UK company) is caught when it places the
system on the EU market, has EU tenants, or when the system's **output is used in the EU**
(extraterritorial hook). A purely UK customer base keeps most obligations at arm's length
(the UK has no AI Act; principles-based regime) — but an open self-serve product cannot
assume that, and a UK tenant's customers may themselves be in the EU. **Working posture:
comply as if in scope.** Compliance is also a sales asset for the honesty-first brand.

**BLAM8's role:** *provider* of an AI system (downstream integrator of Anthropic's GPAI —
Anthropic carries the GPAI-provider duties). Tenants (the businesses) are *deployers*.
Some duties land on them, which BLAM8 should make easy to meet (see §5).

## 2. Risk classification

| Category | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Prohibited practices (Art 5, in force Feb 2025) | **Not engaged.** No manipulation, social scoring, biometric categorisation, emotion recognition in work/education. |
| High-risk (Annex III, applying from Aug 2026/2027) | **Not engaged today.** Email drafting, booking/scheduling, TMS/FSM ops are not Annex III uses. **⚠ Roadmap landmine:** the planned *recruitment vertical* — Annex III 4(a) covers AI used to "analyse and filter job applications [or] evaluate candidates". If that vertical ever screens/ranks candidates, BLAM8 enters the full high-risk regime (conformity assessment, QMS, technical documentation, registration). Design it to schedule interviews and handle correspondence, NOT to evaluate candidates — or accept the high-risk compliance load knowingly. |

| GPAI (Ch V, since Aug 2025) | Sits with Anthropic. Keep their model documentation / usage terms on file. |
| AI literacy (Art 4, since Feb 2025) | Applies lightly: document staff competence; give deployers guidance. |

## 3. Article 50 — the concrete comparison

The Commission's draft guidelines confirm **AI agents fall within Art 50(1)**, and where a
provider cannot reliably predict whether the agent will interact with a human, it should be
designed to **disclose its AI nature in every such situation**.

| Surface | Obligation | BLAM8 today | Gap / action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Site/app assistant (concierge)** | 50(1): people must know they're talking to AI (unless obvious) | Widget footer already reads "AI assistant · may be imperfect · nothing sends without your approval"; greeting says "I'm the BLAM8 assistant" | **Near-compliant.** Make it explicit: greeting says "AI assistant" in the first message on every mode. Low effort. |
| **Draft-first email replies** (default mode) | 50(1) targets systems *interacting directly* with people | A human operator reads, can edit, and approves every send — the communication is the business's, assisted by AI (analogous to the guidelines' "deliberate examination of the substance" standard for meaningful human review; BLAM8's approval is substantive, not a rubber stamp — the operator sees the full draft and thumbs-down/edit tools) | **Defensible position: draft-first = human communication tool, no disclosure duty.** Document this reasoning. Keep the approval step substantive (never auto-approve on timer, never bury the draft). |
| **Full-auto email replies** | 50(1): the agent IS interacting directly with the recipient; not "obvious from context" when signed in the business's voice | No AI disclosure on outbound auto-sent mail; capabilities page currently frames disclosure as the customer's policy choice | **GAP — the one real product change.** Add an AI-disclosure line to auto-sent replies (e.g. a footer: "This reply was generated by an AI assistant on behalf of [business]"), default ON when full-auto is enabled; at minimum, mandatory for EU-facing tenants. Update /capabilities wording to reflect it. Target: before 2 Aug 2026. |
| **Machine-readable marking of AI output** | 50(2): providers of generative systems must mark outputs as AI-generated in machine-readable form (Code of Practice pending; AI Omnibus may soften for existing systems) | Nothing | **Cheap insurance:** add an email header (e.g. `X-AI-Generated: BLAM8`) on auto-sent mail. Revisit when the Code of Practice finalises. |
| **Public-interest text publication** | 50(4) deployer duty (disclosure unless human editorial review) | N/A — business correspondence is not "text informing the public on matters of public interest" | No action. |
| **Deepfakes / synthetic media** | 50(2)/(4) | BLAM8 generates none | No action. |

## 4. Adjacent obligations (already largely handled)

- **GDPR / UK GDPR** — privacy policy + sub-processor list public; UK DB / EU compute / US
  AI topology honestly stated. Keep the "never claim UK-only" rule.
- **Logging / accountability** — the Agent Log (every agent action per tenant) is exactly
  the audit trail regulators like; keep it.
- **Human oversight by design** — draft-first default, escalation rules, never-rules,
  hard intake gates: BLAM8's architecture is unusually aligned with the Act's philosophy.
  Say so in marketing; it's true.
