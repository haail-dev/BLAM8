# Recorded Goals in runtime context

Goals saved during About you now reach email and SMS reply prompts, proactive
message prompts, later onboarding/help context, typed/voice business Assistant
prompts and draft diagnosis. They remain separate from the business brief and
writing style. The complete stored text is carried without a new excerpt limit,
including fictional/test qualifications and responsibilities retained by the team.
Mailbox purpose and voice overrides do not replace tenant Goals.

Settings shows recorded Goals as read-only context and links to the existing
drafting/sending controls. Reading Goals performs no write, enables no module and
changes no action permissions. Grounding searches identify matching Goals as
recorded intent, not proof that a control was applied or an action occurred.

The following distinctions remain essential:

| Owner intent | Existing control or limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Stop producing drafts | Drafting hold; this differs from reviewing drafts before sending. |
| Review replies before sending | Review sending mode; mailbox-specific overrides must also be reviewed. |
| Guide wording and promises | Always/Never/Anything else writing guidance. These fields do not disable tools. |
| Keep diary decisions with the team | Recording this sentence does not disable diary mutations. A supported, explicitly approved execution restriction is still required. |
| Require a contractor approval workflow | A Goals sentence does not create that workflow or establish that approval happened. |

This change does not translate prose into controls or introduce a general policy
engine. In particular it does not provide an agent-only diary restriction or a
tenant-wide reply-review constraint overriding every mailbox and background
send. Those require a separate reviewed implementation with durable actor/source
and version receipts, checks before staging and execution (including bulk and
queued work), fresh checks at send/confirmation boundaries and tests that manual
work stays available. Existing tenants receive no automatic policy backfill.

Offline tests exercise the actual prompt builders across reply/proactive and
email/SMS surfaces, typed/voice context, the read-only tenant snapshot, grounding
authority and mailbox inheritance. They verify context transport and unchanged
settings, not model obedience or enforcement of prose restrictions.
