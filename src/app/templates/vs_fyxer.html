BLAM8 vs Fyxer AI: a tidier inbox, or an answered business?

Fyxer and BLAM8 both learn your voice from your sent mail and draft replies that sound like you. Up close they're solving different problems. Fyxer makes one professional faster at their own inbox. BLAM8 runs the business's inbox: it learns your voice and grounds every reply in your real prices, books real jobs, and fills in the CRM as it goes.

When Fyxer AI is the right choice

  • ✓You're an executive, consultant or recruiter drowning in your own correspondence
  • ✓You want your mailbox sorted into "to respond", "FYI" and "marketing", plus drafts in your tone, inside Gmail or Outlook with no setup
  • ✓You'd value Fyxer Chat, which searches your inbox, meetings and files and writes emails, and a meeting notetaker in the same subscription

Personal productivity is Fyxer's game and it's a good one, from a strong UK brand. BLAM8 doesn't sort your personal inbox or take meeting notes.

When BLAM8 is the right choice

  • ✓The inbox that matters is the business's, like info@, bookings@ or orders@, where every email is a customer
  • ✓Replies must quote your actual prices and policies, from a knowledge base you control, not whatever old emails imply
  • ✓You want the job behind the email done: booked into a live diary, quoted, and the CRM filled in by itself

Fyxer stops at the draft, by design. BLAM8 starts there.

The same email, through both

A customer writes to your business address: "Hi, what would you charge to service two units, and could anyone come Thursday morning?"

In Fyxer

The thread is labelled "To Respond" and a draft appears in the mailbox, written in your tone, just like BLAM8 would. But the price in it is whatever your past emails suggest, not a curated rate card. If your last email mentioned a discount, the draft echoes it. Thursday morning is a suggestion of free time on your own calendar, not a held slot. Whoever owns the seat still checks the number, fixes the quote, and books the job somewhere else.

In BLAM8

The draft quotes the two-unit service off your actual rate card, offers Thursday 9–11 with the right team member, and holds that slot so nobody can double-book it while the customer decides. On Pro, the contact, the deal and the promise are already in the CRM. You approve: sent, booked, tracked.

What Fyxer reviewers raise, and how BLAM8 handles it

Emails lost in the auto-filing. Some reviewers say mail went missing once Fyxer sorted it. BLAM8's labels mark where each job stands, like Needs Reply, Replied or Booked, and you can change any label in one click.

Seats billed when the trial ends. Reviewers report the trial charging for every seat that was invited. BLAM8 is one price per business, with no seats, and the trial needs no card.

Fixed categories, little control. BLAM8's labels are set too, but they follow the job rather than the sender, and what the agent says comes from a knowledge base you write and correct.

Where BLAM8 pulls ahead

BLAM8 isn't just an email assistant: it's a front desk. On Pro + Concierge, you can chat or talk to the Concierge to draft emails, block days, change a stay or hand a job to your team, each action confirmed by you before it happens. Smart inbox labels organise every email without setup, and because they track the job, not just the mail, "Needs Reply" becomes "Draft Ready" becomes "Replied" on its own. Your existing customers can check and move their bookings on their own page. And it all installs as an app with push notifications, no app store.

Fyxer is a good email assistant, and Fyxer Chat and its meeting notetaker are edges BLAM8 doesn't match. But on grounding, booking and the CRM, Fyxer stops at the inbox, and BLAM8 is only getting started there.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Fyxer AI and BLAM8?+

They automate different inboxes. Fyxer is a personal email assistant: it sorts an individual professional's mailbox into categories, drafts replies in their tone learned from their sent mail, and Fyxer Chat searches their inbox, meetings and files. It's priced per seat and aimed at executives and knowledge workers. BLAM8 is a business agent: it runs the company mailbox (info@, bookings@), grounds every reply in your actual prices, policies and services from a knowledge base you control, labels every email by where the job stands (Needs Reply, Draft Ready, Replied, Booked, FYI, Automated), books real jobs into a live diary with holds that can't double-book, and, on Pro, fills in a CRM from the mail. On Pro + Concierge you also get the Concierge, an assistant you can chat or talk to that takes confirmed actions.

Does Fyxer AI know my prices and policies?+

Fyxer learns how you write from your sent mail. BLAM8 does the same, one click at setup, but it also learns what your business charges. BLAM8 drafts from both your writing style and a knowledge base you curate: your rate card, services and policies. A Fyxer draft sounds right but may quote the wrong price. A BLAM8 draft sounds right and quotes the right price. When BLAM8 doesn't know the answer, it holds the email and asks you rather than guess.

Can Fyxer AI book appointments or jobs?+

It proposes meeting times from your calendar and offers scheduling links, which is genuinely useful for meetings. It doesn't hold slots, prevent double-booking beyond suggesting free times, or know about jobs, staff, resources, quotes or invoices. BLAM8 books the work itself, into a live diary, with holds. It never books a date or a time nobody chose: a missing detail holds the space and asks.

Can Fyxer run a shared business mailbox like info@?+

Fyxer is built per seat, for an individual's own mailboxes, and isn't designed as a shared operational inbox for a team. BLAM8 is the opposite: the business mailbox is the whole point, with every conversation, booking and CRM record belonging to the company, not a seat.
