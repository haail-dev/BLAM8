The mechanics, stated plainly

Everything below is a term of the agreement you sign — not marketing copy. Read the full agreement before you join, it's short.

Lifetime means lifetime

Commission is payable for the life of the referred account — every payment while they remain subscribed. Upgrades raise it; downgrades lower it; it never expires while they're active.

{{ window_days }}-day conversion window

An introduction must become a paid subscription within {{ window_days }} days of the referral. Once that first payment lands in-window, the lifetime entitlement is locked in.

Three ways attribution sticks

Your link, your code typed at signup, or an introduction registered on your partner page before they sign up. First attribution wins and is never rewritten.

Paid monthly

A statement every month, payment within 30 days of the month's end, in GBP. Balances under £{{ min_payout }} simply roll forward — nothing is forfeited.

Earned when collected

Commission exists only when the business actually pays. Free trials earn nothing until they convert; refunds and chargebacks are deducted from your next statement.

No self-referral

Your own business — or one you control — doesn't qualify. Use BLAM8 yourself by all means; it just doesn't pay you commission.
