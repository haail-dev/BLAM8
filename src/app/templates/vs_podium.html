Does Podium handle email?+

As a side channel. Podium can bring Gmail and Outlook threads into its inbox, but the product is text-first: the AI Employee's home ground is texts, calls and webchat, and email automation isn't the pitch. BLAM8 is the mirror image: email-first, with drafts in your voice grounded in your own prices and policies, and jobs booked into a live diary.

Does BLAM8 do reviews like Podium?+

No. Review generation and reputation management are Podium's original core, and BLAM8 doesn't compete there today. BLAM8's job is the conversation and the diary: enquiries answered, jobs booked, promises chased. If reviews tooling is your main need, Podium (in its markets) or a UK reviews tool is the right category.

Does the AI answer live, or do I approve first?+

With BLAM8, you choose. Every account starts approval-first, with drafts waiting for you, and you flip to full-auto per category once it's earned trust. Podium's AI Employee answers live, with human handoff as the escalation route. Jerry 2.0 lets you see its reasoning and coach it in plain English, which is a good idea. BLAM8's version is the approval queue: thumbs-down a line once and it becomes a standing rule, and if it doesn't know an answer it holds the email and asks you.

Does BLAM8 have webchat like Podium?+

No, BLAM8 doesn't put a chat box on your website. The front door is the email: a customer writes, the reply offers what the diary really has, and a yes books it. It never books a date or a time nobody chose. Your existing customers, the ones your emails already reach, can also check, move and pay their bookings and ask questions by conversation at blam8.com/c/your-business. They get in with the reference code on every email, or a code sent to the address you hold. Strangers are turned away.

Does BLAM8 answer calls or text back missed calls like Podium?+

BLAM8 doesn't answer phone calls. It answers email and runs the diary. SMS and missed-call text-back are built and being tested; they switch on when testing is done. Until then, if your leads live on the phone, Podium's calls-and-texts product is doing a job BLAM8 doesn't do yet.
