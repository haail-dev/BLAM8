1. Parties

This Partner Referral Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between BLAM8 ("Company"), an AI-powered back-office operating system for small and medium-sized businesses, and the partner named in the enrolment form ("Partner"). Together referred to as the "Parties".

2. Purpose

The Partner agrees to introduce and refer prospective customers to BLAM8. In return, BLAM8 agrees to pay the Partner a recurring commission on all subscription revenue generated from successfully referred customers, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

3. Commission Structure

3.1 Rate: The Partner will receive 20% of the subscription fees actually collected from each referred customer, excluding any VAT or sales taxes and net of any discounts, refunds and chargebacks. Payment processing fees are not deducted.

3.2 Duration: Commission is payable for the life of the referred customer's account — meaning for as long as that customer maintains an active, paying subscription with BLAM8. There is no expiry on commission entitlement while the referred customer remains active.

3.3 Eligibility: A referral is eligible for commission when: (a) the referred customer is new and not previously known to or in discussion with BLAM8; (b) the Partner is identified as the referral source at the point of sign-up via referral link or prior written notification; and (c) the referred customer completes and maintains a paid subscription.

3.4 No commission cap: There is no limit on the number of customers the Partner may refer or the total commission earnable under this Agreement.

3.5 Subscription changes: If a referred customer upgrades or downgrades their subscription tier, the Partner's 20% commission will adjust accordingly based on the new subscription value.

3.6 Scope of commission: Commission applies to BLAM8's standard published subscription plans and recurring add-ons (including additional mailboxes). Where a referred customer signs a bespoke or individually negotiated contract (including Enterprise plans), the commission rate for that customer will be agreed between the Parties in writing at the time.

3.7 Self-referral: Subscriptions purchased by the Partner for its own business, or for any business in which the Partner holds a controlling interest, do not qualify for commission under this Agreement.

3.8 Future rate changes: BLAM8 may vary the commission rate applicable to future referrals by giving 30 days' written notice. Customers referred before such a change remain commissioned at the rate in force on the date of their referral, for the life of their account.

4. Payment

4.1 Commissions will be calculated monthly based on confirmed subscription payments received from referred customers during that calendar month.

4.2 Payment will be made within 30 days of the end of each calendar month in which commission was earned.

4.3 Settlement method: The Partner may elect, on their partner page, to receive commission either (a) by bank transfer, or (b) as credit against the Partner's own BLAM8 subscription. Bank transfer applies unless the Partner elects otherwise. The Partner may change this election at any time; a change applies to commission settled after the change and does not affect commission already settled. Where commission is paid by bank transfer, banking details will be agreed separately in writing prior to the first payment.

4.4 BLAM8 will provide the Partner with a monthly commission statement detailing referred customers, subscription amounts, and commission earned.

4.5 The Partner is solely responsible for any income tax, VAT, or other taxes applicable to commission in their jurisdiction, however that commission is settled — including where it is applied as subscription credit under Clause 4.9 rather than paid in cash. BLAM8 will not withhold tax unless required by law.

4.6 Minimum threshold: Where total commission earned in a given month is less than £10, BLAM8 reserves the right to roll the balance forward to the following month.

4.7 Refunds and chargebacks: Where a subscription payment on which commission has been paid is subsequently refunded, charged back, or otherwise not collected, the corresponding commission will be deducted from the Partner's next commission statement. If no further commission becomes payable, BLAM8 may invoice the Partner for the amount, payable within 30 days.

4.8 Currency: All commission payments will be made in pounds sterling (GBP) unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4.9 Subscription credit: Where the Partner has elected subscription credit under Clause 4.3, BLAM8 will apply cleared commission as a credit to the Partner's own BLAM8 subscription account, reducing the amount payable on that account's next invoice. Subscription credit is available only to a Partner who holds their own BLAM8 subscription. A credit cannot reduce an invoice below zero; any excess remains on the Partner's account and is applied against subsequent invoices. Because commission is settled in whole amounts, BLAM8 may apply the whole commission amount that meets or first exceeds the invoice value, with any excess carried forward as above. For the avoidance of doubt, applying commission earned from referred customers to the Partner's own subscription is not a self-referral within the meaning of Clause 3.7.

4.10 Withdrawal of accumulated commission: The Partner may request payment in cash of commission that has cleared and is not retained as cover under Clause 4.11. The minimum amount per request is £50, and only one request may be open at any time. Because commission is settled in whole amounts, the sum actually paid may be less than the sum requested; the amount settled is shown on the Partner's statement. BLAM8 will pay a valid request within 30 days of the request.

4.11 Retained cover: BLAM8 may retain a portion of cleared commission as cover against refunds and chargebacks falling due under Clause 4.7. The amount retained is one month of the Partner's own subscription fee plus 20% of the Partner's monthly commission run-rate, and will not exceed three times that subscription fee. Retained cover is not a deduction and is not forfeited: it remains commission earned by and owed to the Partner, is shown on the Partner's partner page, and is released as it ceases to be required. On termination of this Agreement for any reason other than the Partner's material breach, any remaining retained cover is released with the Partner's final statement.

4.12 Invoicing and VAT: The Partner is responsible for determining the correct VAT treatment of commission in their own circumstances, whether it is paid in cash or applied as subscription credit. Where the Partner is registered for VAT, BLAM8 may require a valid VAT invoice from the Partner before making payment, and may agree self-billing arrangements with the Partner in writing.

5. Referral Process & Attribution

5.1 The Partner will be issued a unique referral link or code to identify and track referrals attributed to them.

5.2 Where a referral link is not used, the Partner must notify BLAM8 in writing (email is acceptable, and registering the introduction on the Partner's BLAM8 partner page constitutes written notification) of an introduction prior to the prospect signing up, in order for attribution to be established.

5.3 In the event of a dispute over attribution between two or more partners, BLAM8 will award commission to the partner who first made written contact or whose referral link was used at point of sign-up. BLAM8's decision on attribution disputes is final.

5.4 Referral attribution is valid for 90 days from the date the prospect was first introduced. If the prospect does not convert to a paid subscription within 90 days, attribution lapses unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6. Partner Obligations

6.1 The Partner agrees to represent BLAM8 accurately and professionally, and not to make claims about BLAM8's features, pricing, or capabilities beyond those set out in official BLAM8 materials.

6.2 The Partner is an independent contractor and is not an employee, agent, joint venture partner, or legal representative of BLAM8. The Partner has no authority to enter into contracts, make commitments, or incur liabilities on BLAM8's behalf.

6.3 The Partner must not engage in any misleading, deceptive, or high-pressure sales practices when introducing BLAM8 to prospective customers.

6.4 The Partner must comply with all applicable laws and regulations in their jurisdiction when carrying out referral activities, including data protection laws.

6.5 The Partner must not bid on or purchase search-engine advertising against the BLAM8 brand name or confusingly similar terms, and must not register domain names or social media handles incorporating the BLAM8 name, without BLAM8's prior written consent.

6.6 The Partner must not send bulk or unsolicited electronic marketing (including email or SMS) referencing BLAM8. All referral marketing must comply with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and any equivalent legislation applicable in the Partner's jurisdiction.

7.2 The Partner may use BLAM8's brand name and approved marketing materials solely for the purpose of making introductions under this Agreement. Any other use requires prior written consent from BLAM8.

8. Data Protection

8.1 Both Parties agree to comply with all applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

8.2 Where the Partner shares personal data of prospective customers with BLAM8 for the purpose of referral, the Partner warrants that they have a lawful basis for doing so and that the individual has been informed their details may be passed to BLAM8.

8.3 Neither Party will use personal data obtained under this Agreement for any purpose beyond what is necessary to fulfil the terms of this Agreement.

9.1 Both Parties agree to keep the terms of this Agreement and any commercially sensitive information shared between the Parties confidential, and not to disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

9.2 This obligation of confidentiality survives termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.

9.3 Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) is required to be disclosed by law or court order; or (c) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure.

10.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months following termination, the Partner agrees not to directly solicit, approach, or attempt to divert any BLAM8 customer (including referred customers) to a competing product or service without BLAM8's prior written consent.

11.2 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, special, or consequential loss, loss of profit, loss of revenue, or loss of business arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such loss.

11.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits either Party's liability for fraud, death, or personal injury caused by negligence, or any other liability that cannot be excluded by law.

12. Indemnification

12.1 The Partner agrees to indemnify and hold harmless BLAM8, its directors, employees, and agents from and against any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from: (a) the Partner's breach of this Agreement; (b) any misrepresentation made by the Partner about BLAM8; or (c) the Partner's violation of any applicable law in carrying out referral activities.

13. Term & Termination

13.1 This Agreement begins on the date of the Partner's electronic acceptance and continues indefinitely until terminated.

13.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

13.3 BLAM8 may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if the Partner: (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement; (b) engages in conduct likely to bring BLAM8 into disrepute; or (c) becomes insolvent or enters administration.

13.4 On termination for any reason other than Partner material breach, commission already accrued on active referred accounts will continue to be paid for as long as those customers remain active paying BLAM8 subscribers.

13.5 On termination due to Partner material breach, BLAM8 reserves the right to withhold unpaid commission pending resolution of the breach.

14. Dispute Resolution

14.1 In the event of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Parties agree to first attempt to resolve the matter through good-faith negotiation within 14 days of the dispute being raised in writing.

14.2 If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the Parties agree to attempt mediation before initiating legal proceedings. The cost of mediation shall be shared equally between the Parties unless otherwise agreed.

14.3 If mediation fails, either Party may pursue resolution through the courts as set out in Clause 15.
