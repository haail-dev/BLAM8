BLAM8 vs Jobber: who answers the email that starts the job?

Jobber is a serious field service platform: quotes, crews, invoices, payments and a client portal. But it manages the job after it exists. The enquiry that starts it lands in your inbox, and Jobber doesn't read your inbox: replies to its emails go back to your ordinary mailbox and stay there. BLAM8 starts at that inbox: every enquiry answered in your voice, triaged, quoted off your rate card, and booked.

When Jobber is the right choice

  • ✓You run big crews and your bottleneck is dispatch: drag-and-drop calendars, GPS routing, a field app for the team
  • ✓You want a mature payments stack: card on file, in-field collection, batch invoicing, a self-serve client portal
  • ✓Enquiries mostly arrive by phone or web form, not email, and the AI Receptionist add-on covers calls and texts (US and Canada-centred)

Jobber has a polished scheduling-to-invoice flow and over a decade of field-ops depth. This page won't pretend otherwise.

When BLAM8 is the right choice

  • ✓Your jobs start as emails, and answering them evenings and weekends is the real admin burden
  • ✓You want the reply drafted in your voice, priced off your rate card, and the job booked in the same thread, approval-first
  • ✓You're a UK business and want GBP pricing per business, not per user, so the bill doesn't grow with the crew

BLAM8 books the job from the email itself and keeps the CRM written: the front door Jobber doesn't have.

Frequently asked questions

Can Jobber answer my customer emails?+

No. Jobber sends emails out (quotes, invoices, reminders) and logs them on the client record, but replies go to your ordinary company inbox and aren't pulled back into Jobber. There's no two-way email inbox, and no AI that answers inbound enquiries from your business knowledge. Jobber's AI helps the owner (Copilot, quote drafts, message polish), and its AI Receptionist add-on answers phone calls and texts. BLAM8 starts exactly where Jobber stops: it reads every inbound email, drafts a reply in your voice from your own prices and policies, and books the job.

How does Jobber pricing work for UK businesses?+

Jobber's pricing page says all prices are in USD, and its /uk address leads there. For one user: Core $49/month ($29 billed annually), Connect $139 ($99 annually) and Grow $199 ($149 annually). First-year promos run lower. Prices rise as you add users, so the bill grows with the crew. Add-ons include the AI Receptionist at $29/month for 30 conversations, then $0.79 each. There's no GBP price list, so UK businesses carry the exchange rate, and UK needs like CIS and MTD VAT still need Xero or QuickBooks alongside. Does Jobber book jobs automatically?+

Through self-serve: customers can pick a slot on an online booking form or request work through the Client Hub, and the AI Receptionist add-on can book from a phone call or text. Nothing books from an email conversation. If the enquiry arrives as an email, a person reads it, replies, and either sends a form link or keys the job in. BLAM8 books from the email itself: it checks the live diary, holds a slot that can't be double-booked, and confirms in the same thread once you approve. It never books a date or a time nobody chose.

Is BLAM8 a Jobber alternative?+

For a UK trades business whose work arrives by email, yes. BLAM8 covers the front of the job: the enquiry answered in your voice and the job booked into a live diary from {{ product_price('email') }}/month in GBP, with quotes and invoices on every plan and the self-filling CRM on the {{ product_price('starter') }}/month Pro plan. Jobber stays deeper on field operations at scale, like batch invoicing, crew GPS and payments hardware, and if those are your bottleneck it's a fine tool. The difference is who answers the email that starts the job.

Is BLAM8 as deep on field operations as Jobber?+

Honestly: no, and it doesn't try to be. Jobber has a decade of dispatch, payments and client-portal depth, and BLAM8 doesn't match that. What BLAM8 does is answer the enquiry, book the job and fill in the CRM. That's the front door Jobber doesn't have at any tier: the inbox, answered.

Can I use BLAM8 with Jobber?+

There's no direct integration today, but nothing stops BLAM8 answering the inbox while Jobber runs your field operations. If you need the full job lifecycle, like dispatch, invoicing at scale and in-field payments, run both. If what you need is the inbox answered and the diary filled, BLAM8 on its own may be enough. SMS and missed-call text-back are built and being tested; they switch on when testing is done.
