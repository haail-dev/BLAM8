How loud counts as you

Talk as you normally would. Grey is the room, blue is you. Set the line above the grey and below the blue: a request quieter than the line is held, and the screen asks you to repeat it.

Talk as you normally would. Grey is the room, blue is you. With the line set, sound quieter than the line is not sent to the agent. Off, everything is sent and the agent decides what is speech. If this phone cannot measure the room, everything is sent and the voice screen says so. Takes effect from the next conversation.

Starting the mic…

The line is not used while Hold to talk is on.

How you talk

Hold to talk keeps background sound from reaching the agent between your turns. Press and hold Mist to speak, release when you have finished. A short tap keeps the mic open until you tap again. End the conversation with the End button. Takes effect from the next conversation. Saved on this device.

Conversation mode

Switching ends the current conversation. GPT-Live uses native audio and the same business agent. Business results and confirmations remain in chat.

Voice volume

How loud the Concierge speaks, on top of your phone's volume. Takes effect from the next conversation.

Greeting

Whether the Concierge says a word ("Morning.") when the voice screen opens. Off, it opens quietly and listens straight away. Saved on this device.

Launch by button

Start listening automatically when opened from a launch button. This works on some phones. If yours needs a tap you will still see the mic button, so tap it. It only applies to a link ending in #talk, never to a refresh or a notification. Saved on this device.

How to set up a launch button

iPhone (not tested on a device yet)

  1. Install BLAM8 to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen).
  2. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with one action, Open URLs, set to https://blam8.com/app/concierge#talk
  3. Settings, Action Button, Shortcut, and pick that shortcut.

Found on an iPhone 16 Pro Max: an https link in the Shortcuts app opens a tab in your default browser, not the installed app, though it does reach the voice screen. Unofficial, device-specific alternative: an Open URLs action with webapp://blam8.com/app/concierge#talk opens the installed web app, but only if you added BLAM8 to the Home Screen from Safari, and it opens whichever Home Screen web app is there, so keep BLAM8 the only one. It ignores the address and lands on Today, so turn on “Open on the voice screen” above. Whether iOS reloads or just resumes the app on the button press is untested; the setting handles both, but resuming only counts after the app has been away for ten minutes. On iPhone you will normally still need to tap the mic once, because iOS will not play sound until you touch the screen.

Android (untested)

  • Long-press the BLAM8 icon and choose Talk to Concierge.
  • Pixel: Quick Tap (double-tap the back) can open the app. Untested.
  • Samsung: a side-key option may exist. Untested.

A Bluetooth headset's own button cannot launch BLAM8. Once the voice screen is open, the headset carries the sound.

Open on the voice screen

When you open the installed app from your Home Screen (or a launch button), land on the voice screen, ready for a tap on the mic. It only happens on the first open of a launch, never when you move around inside the app, reload, or tap a notification. With “Launch by button” also On it tries to start listening by itself. Saved on this device.
