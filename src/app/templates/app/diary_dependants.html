Records linked to a customer

A customer may have several people, places or items you need to keep track of separately. Name the type of record and choose which details to keep for each one. The assistant can then collect those details and link them to the right customer.

When details are collected
  • Ask once — asked when missing, then reused from the saved record.
  • Confirm each time — prefilled and re-confirmed in one line ("is that still correct?").
  • Expires — a date that lapses (an expiring certificate); tick must cover the booking and a lapsed one blocks that one, not the others.
