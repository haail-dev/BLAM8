How it works

Groups every completed job in the period by customer — one invoice each, priced from the job's quote (or the rate card). What appears on the invoice (VAT, your business details, terms) is set in Getting paid. Work that's already been invoiced never appears, so generating twice can't double-bill anyone.

About the export formats

The accountant format is the safe default — it opens in any spreadsheet and carries every field, including who’s paid. The Xero and QuickBooks formats match their invoice import. Both need an account code and a tax rate that exist in your chart of accounts, so check those in the file before importing — we default to Xero’s standard Sales code (200) and can’t see your setup.
