Coming from Xero? Two exports bring your world across in one sitting — no field mapping, no setup. Anything that can't be read is counted and reported, never silently dropped.

Contacts

In Xero: Contacts → Export. Your customers land in the CRM with their names and emails — the self-filling starts from a full address book instead of an empty one.

Invoices

In Xero: Business → Invoices → Export. Your invoice numbers are kept exactly as they are (your accountant matches on them), totals come across as-is, and anything unpaid joins the chase list. Xero's drafts and voided invoices stay behind.

One-time and safe to re-run — anything already here is skipped, never duplicated.

The other half of the promise: you can always leave with everything — one download, and it works even if you cancel.
