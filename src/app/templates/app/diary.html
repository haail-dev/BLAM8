    How the diary works

    Click a day to see its bookings; ✕ Close takes you back to the month. Proposed holds are placed by the agent and count against capacity until you (or an approved reply) confirm them — stale ones are released automatically after 7 days. Grey Offered slots are options the agent has put in front of a customer: each is reserved exclusively for them (so nobody is double-offered) until they pick one or the stated deadline passes, when it frees itself.
