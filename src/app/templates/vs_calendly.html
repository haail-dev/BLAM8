BLAM8 vs Calendly: a booking link, or a booked conversation?

Calendly perfected one moment: the customer clicks your link and picks a slot. Its new assistant, Callie, can now book a meeting when you copy it into a thread. But someone still has to answer the rest of the email, and the link only knows meeting availability, not jobs. BLAM8 owns the whole exchange: the email answered in your voice, the right slot held, the job booked, in the same thread. The email is the booking page.

When Calendly is the right choice

  • ✓You book meetings, such as sales calls, demos, interviews and consultations, and invitees are happy to self-serve
  • ✓You run a team that needs round robin, lead routing and CRM handoffs
  • ✓You want a free or very cheap way to publish one person's availability, and a well-known link people trust
  • ✓You want an AI that books meetings from a thread you copy it into (Callie, on the Plus plans) and a meeting notetaker

That's Calendly's home turf, and it's genuinely best-in-class there. It now takes payments, sells packages and sends invoices too. BLAM8 doesn't do round-robin sales routing and isn't trying to.

When BLAM8 is the right choice

  • ✓Customers email about work, like service visits, deliveries, fittings, viewings or stays, and each one needs a proper reply, not just a link
  • ✓What gets scheduled is a job: real capacity, timed slots, whole days or multi-day work, priced from your own prices
  • ✓You want the inbox handled end to end: answered, quoted, booked and followed up, with you approving first
  • ✓You want a booking you can trust: it never books a date or a time nobody chose, and asks when something's missing

With Calendly, the inbox is still your job. With BLAM8, the inbox is the product.

The customer picks from your meeting availability, which knows nothing about how much work Thursday can actually take, or what the job costs. If they don't click, the thread goes quiet.

With BLAM8

Frequently asked questions

Can Calendly answer my customer emails?+

Not the whole email. Calendly's own emails are confirmations and templated reminders. Its new AI assistant, Callie (in beta, on the Plus plans), can be copied into a thread to suggest times, handle the back-and-forth and book a meeting. But if a customer asks what a job costs, whether you cover their area and when you're free, nothing answers the rest of it. BLAM8 starts at the inbox: it reads the email, drafts a reply in your voice from your own prices and policies, and books the job in the same thread.

Calendly has an AI email assistant now, Callie. Isn't that the same?+

Callie books meetings when you copy it into a thread. BLAM8 answers every customer email from your own prices and books jobs against your capacity and rules. Callie is a real step for meeting scheduling, and it's fair to say so. The difference is what gets answered and what gets booked: BLAM8 answers the whole email, and books the job against real capacity, the details it needs and any documents that must be in date.

Does Calendly work for jobs — service visits, deliveries, crews and vans?+

It schedules people's meeting availability, not jobs. There are no job records or travel awareness, just event types with fixed durations and static buffers. Booking a resource like a van, bay or room needs the workaround of creating it as an extra user seat or wiring sub-calendars through Zapier. BLAM8's diary schedules the work itself: timed slots, whole days and multi-day jobs against real capacity, with holds that can't double-book, plus your real prices quoted in the reply.

It's cheap per seat. BLAM8 is priced per business, not per seat: £39/month (Starter, 300 emails), £99/month (Pro, 750 emails) or £249/month (Pro + Concierge, 750 emails). For meetings, such as sales calls, demos and interviews, Calendly is excellent and BLAM8 isn't trying to replace it. But if you've been sending Calendly links to customers who emailed about actual work, like a service visit, a delivery or a fitting, BLAM8 replaces the whole exchange. The email is answered in your voice, the details gathered, and the job booked against your live diary without the customer ever leaving the thread. The email is the booking page.

Your existing customers also get a page at blam8.com/c/your-business where they can check, move and pay their bookings and ask questions by conversation. Can I use both?+

Yes. Plenty of businesses will keep a Calendly link for internal meetings and sales calls while BLAM8 runs the customer inbox and the jobs diary. They only collide if you're using Calendly links as a substitute for answering customers.
