BLAM8 vs Moneypenny: they answer your phone. Who answers your email?

Moneypenny is a well-known UK answering service: people, or an AI Receptionist, picking up your calls and website chats. BLAM8 does a different job. It answers your email and runs the diary: replies in your voice from your real prices, the work booked, the quote and invoice sent. BLAM8 doesn't answer calls. Yes, and for a business that gets both calls and emails it can make sense.
