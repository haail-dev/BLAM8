BLAM8 vs Zapier: buy a worker, or build the plumbing?

Zapier is a genuinely great toolkit — thousands of app integrations, triggers, and actions you wire together yourself. But a toolkit is what it is: you're the builder, and you're the maintenance department. BLAM8 is one job done completely: your email answered in your voice and the bookings behind it placed in your diary — working the day you connect a mailbox.

When Zapier is the right choice

  • ✓You need to connect many different apps: forms to CRMs, spreadsheets to Slack, shops to accounting
  • ✓You enjoy building automations and want full control of every step
  • ✓Your workflows are about moving data between tools, not holding a conversation

That's what Zapier is built for. BLAM8 connects your mailbox and calendar — it is not a general integration platform and doesn't try to be.

When BLAM8 is the right choice

  • ✓The "workflow" you actually need is: answer customer emails properly and get the jobs booked
  • ✓You want it grounded in your real prices, policies and FAQs, with an approval queue, not raw AI
  • ✓You want zero workflows to build or babysit, and control by feedback: thumbs-down a bad line once and it becomes a standing rule

You could approximate some of this in Zapier. You'd be building it, paying per task for it, and fixing it when it breaks.

The same email, through both

A customer writes: "Hi, any chance someone could come out Thursday to take a look? Mornings are best for us."

In Zapier (after you've built the Zap)

The trigger fires, an AI step writes a generic reply (it doesn't know your prices or availability unless you've engineered that), a draft appears in your mailbox, and 2–3 tasks are billed. Nobody holds Thursday morning. If the customer replies, the thread context is whatever you built. If the Zap breaks, it's your Tuesday evening. And every email spends tasks from your monthly allowance.

In BLAM8

A reply is drafted in your voice, quoting your actual services and rates; the Thursday-morning slot is held so you can't double-book; the booking details sit pinned on the thread. You approve: sent and in the diary. Full-auto once it's earned your trust.

Frequently asked questions

Can Zapier answer my customer emails automatically?+

Not out of the box. Zapier is workflow plumbing: you can assemble a Zap (new email → AI step → create draft) yourself, and each inbound email consumes 2–3 billable tasks. What you build has no approval queue, no knowledge base grounding replies in your real prices and policies, no conversation memory, and you maintain it yourself. BLAM8 is that whole system pre-built as a product: connect a mailbox and the next email in gets a draft in your voice from your own business facts.

Does Zapier do appointment scheduling?+

No — Zapier has no diary or availability concept. The standard answer is integrating a booking-link tool such as Calendly (from £8 per seat/month billed yearly), where the customer leaves the email, opens a link, and self-serves against one person's meeting availability. Nothing decides an email is a booking request or holds a slot. BLAM8 books from the conversation itself: it checks your live diary, places a hold that can't double-book, and confirms in the same thread.

How does Zapier pricing compare to BLAM8?+

Zapier bills by task (one action step) and shows USD to UK visitors: there's a Free plan, Professional starts at $19.99/month billed annually, and Team at $69/month billed annually, with the price rising as your task allowance grows. AI agents are a separate add-on. A DIY email-answering Zap costs 2–3 tasks per email, so 300 emails a month is 600–900 tasks before any AI costs, and you still need a booking tool. BLAM8's £39/month Starter plan includes the drafting AI, knowledge base, approval queue and live-diary booking as a finished product, for 300 emails. Prices checked September 2026.

Is BLAM8 a Zapier alternative?+

Only for one job. Zapier connects thousands of apps and automates workflows across them — BLAM8 doesn't compete with that and never will. But if the workflow you were about to build in Zapier is 'answer my customer emails and book the jobs', BLAM8 is that workflow as a finished product: answered in your voice, booking details gathered into a pinned card, and booked into your live diary, on every plan.

Can I use both?+

Absolutely. Keep Zapier for moving data between your other tools, and let BLAM8 own the inbox and the diary. They meet different needs, which is exactly why this page exists.
