BLAM8 vs Tradify: job software per user, or the email answered?

Tradify is popular with UK trades for good reason: scheduling, quotes, invoices, timesheets and certificates in one app. What it doesn't do is answer the email that starts the job. BLAM8 sits on the mailbox you already have, replies in your voice from your real prices, and books the work into a live diary, for one price per business rather than per person.

When Tradify is the right choice

  • ✓Your crew needs timesheets, certificates and forms, and purchase orders on site
  • ✓You want the job run in the van: job cards, quotes, invoices and card payments on the spot
  • ✓You want live sync with Xero, Sage or QuickBooks Online
  • ✓Most of your work arrives already agreed, by phone or in person, so the inbox isn't where the time goes

Tradify is good at the site end of the job. This page won't pretend otherwise.

When BLAM8 is the right choice

  • ✓Your jobs start as emails, and answering them in the evening is the real admin
  • ✓You want the reply drafted in your voice from your own prices, the date held in the diary, and you just approve
  • ✓Your team is growing and you'd rather not pay for every person: people, including staff who never sign in, cost nothing extra
  • ✓You want the customer record filled in from the emails, not typed

BLAM8 answers the email Tradify never sees. The job arrives already booked.

Frequently asked questions

Can Tradify answer my customer emails?+

Not for you. Tradify's AI tools, which it calls SmartTools, come on the Plus plan only and help with things like reading supplier bills and writing. None of its plans answers the enquiries arriving in your mailbox, so each reply is still yours to write. BLAM8 connects to the Outlook or Gmail inbox you already have, drafts every reply in your voice from your own prices and policies, holds the date in a live diary and waits for your approval.

How much does Tradify cost in the UK?+

On 25 September 2026 Tradify's UK pricing page showed Lite at £34, Pro at £37 and Plus at £44, each per user per month, with a 14-day free trial. The bill grows with every person you add. BLAM8 is priced per business, not per user: Starter {{ product_price('email') }}, Pro {{ product_price('starter') }} (adds the self-filling CRM) and Pro + Concierge {{ product_price('concierge') }} a month, and your team costs nothing extra.

Does BLAM8 do timesheets, certificates or purchase orders?+

No. Timesheets, certificates and forms, purchase orders and running the job on site are Tradify's ground, and it does them well. BLAM8 covers the front of the job: the enquiry answered, the date booked, the quote and invoice sent and chased, and the customer record filled in by itself on Pro.

Can I use BLAM8 and Tradify together?+

Yes. There's no direct link between them today, but they can run side by side: BLAM8 answers the inbox and books the work, and Tradify keeps the timesheets, certificates and purchase orders. If what's eating your evenings is typing replies, start with BLAM8.

Is BLAM8 a Tradify alternative?+

For part of the job. Both do scheduling, quotes and invoices. Tradify goes deeper on site: timesheets, certificates, purchase orders and live accounting sync. BLAM8 goes deeper on the inbox: every enquiry answered in your voice, bookings that are never made for a date nobody chose, and a CRM that fills itself in. Pick by where your time goes.
